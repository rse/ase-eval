#   REQS: Solution Vision (SV)

-   PURPOSE: Let the employees of the office book its meeting rooms themselves, instead of
    requesting every booking by mail from [[TERM:Facility Management]].
-   TARGET-AUDIENCE: The employees of the office who need a meeting room, and
    [[TERM:Facility Management]], which can cancel any booking.
-   SOLUTION: A web-based, self-service room booking service for the meeting rooms of the office.
-   VALUE-PROPOSITION: An [[TERM:Employee]] finds a [[TERM:Room]] which is free in the needed
    [[TERM:Slot]] and offers the needed capacity and [[TERM:Equipment]], and books it directly.
-   DIFFERENTIATORS: Unlike the status quo of mailing [[TERM:Facility Management]] for every
    booking, the employees see the free rooms themselves and book, check in, and cancel without
    any mail round-trip.
-   SCOPE: The first release covers only the 14 meeting rooms on the 3 floors of the Vienna
    office. A [[TERM:Desk]] is not bookable, and recurring bookings are deferred
    ([[Book a Recurring Slot]]).
-   FUTURE-STATE: The employees book the meeting rooms of the office themselves, and
    [[TERM:Facility Management]] no longer handles booking requests by mail.

The room booking service replaces the booking by mail through Facility Management with the
self-service booking by the employees, BECAUSE product and Facility Management agreed on this
goal and on a first release for the Vienna office only at the kick-off meeting of 2026-04-14.
