# Rubric: Devil's Advocate on a Microservices Rewrite

The output plays Devil's Advocate against the thesis that rewriting a
12-year-old Java monolith as microservices halves the release lead time
within a year. Score each criterion and average them:

1.  **Count**: about four distinct counter-arguments (antitheses), not
    rephrasings of one another.
2.  **Substance**: the counter-arguments are technically sound and
    specific (e.g. distributed-systems overhead, data migration, the
    second-system effect, organizational coupling per Conway's law,
    rewrite risk versus incremental strangler-fig extraction).
3.  **Challenge**: each one genuinely attacks the claim (the lead time,
    the one-year horizon, or the causal link), not a side issue.
4.  **Synthesis**: a closing synthesis weighs the critique and states
    what would make the thesis defensible (or what to do instead).
5.  **Form**: the thesis is restated and the structure is readable; no
    invented statistics presented as facts.
