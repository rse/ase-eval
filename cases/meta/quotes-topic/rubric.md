# Rubric: Quotes on Simplicity in Software Design

The output finds quotes for the keywords "simplicity", "software",
"design" and places them into the skill's 2x2 matrix (with vs. without an
author/origin, and with vs. without a literal keyword). Score each
criterion and average them:

1.  **Count**: about six quotes in total.
2.  **Matrix**: the quotes are sorted into the 2x2 matrix spanned by
    attribution (known author vs. anonymous/proverbial) and literalness
    (contains a keyword literally vs. only conceptually), and the sorting
    is correct for each quote.
3.  **Authenticity**: attributed quotes are real and correctly attributed
    (e.g. Hoare, Dijkstra, Saint-Exupery, Kernighan); doubtful
    attributions are flagged rather than asserted.
4.  **Relevance**: all quotes are about simplicity in design or software.
5.  **Form**: readable presentation, no padding with invented quotes.
