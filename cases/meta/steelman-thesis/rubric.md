# Rubric: Steelman of Mandatory Code Review

The output builds the strongest case for mandatory review of every change,
even one-line typo fixes. Score each criterion and average them:

1.  **Count**: about three distinct supporting arguments (pro-theses).
2.  **Strength**: the arguments are the strongest available (e.g. no
    exception paths to abuse, supply-chain and insider risk, audit and
    compliance requirements, knowledge sharing, cheap with good tooling),
    not strawmen.
3.  **Fortification**: over two rounds, the arguments are visibly
    strengthened against the obvious objections (overhead, latency,
    reviewer fatigue).
4.  **Charity**: the case is argued in good faith, without dismissing
    the objections unfairly or inventing facts.
5.  **Form**: the thesis is restated and the rounds are clearly separated.
