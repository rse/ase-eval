Meeting notes -- room booking service, kick-off (2026-04-14)
Attendees: product (Mia), facility management (Jonas), dev team

Goal: employees book meeting rooms of the office themselves, instead of
mailing facility management. First release only for the Vienna office
(3 floors, 14 rooms).

What people need:
- see which rooms are free in a time slot, filtered by capacity and by
  equipment (screen, video conferencing, whiteboard)
- book a room for a time slot; slots are 15-minute steps, max 4 hours,
  at most 30 days ahead
- one person may hold at most 2 bookings at the same time of day (Jonas:
  people block rooms "just in case")
- cancel own bookings; facility management can cancel any booking and
  must give a reason that is mailed to the booker
- bookings not checked in within 10 minutes after start are released
  automatically (check-in via a tablet at the door, later; for now a
  check-in button in the web UI)

Terms (Jonas insists): a "room" is a bookable meeting room; a "desk" is
NOT part of this; a "booking" is a reservation of one room for one slot by
one employee; a "no-show" is a booking without check-in.

Non-functional: login with the company SSO (OIDC), the booking page must
load in under 1 second, data stays in the EU, availability 99.5% during
office hours (07:00-20:00 local time).

Open: recurring bookings (weekly team meeting) -- not in the first
release, maybe later.
